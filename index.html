About CalorieCoach

The science behind your personalized nutrition plan

🧬

Mifflin-St Jeor Equation

CalorieCoach uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, widely regarded as the most accurate formula for estimating Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). It calculates the calories your body needs at complete rest based on your weight, height, age, and sex.

📊

Understanding Your Numbers

BMR is your resting calorie burn. TDEE multiplies BMR by your activity level. Target Calories adjusts TDEE based on your goal — deficit for weight loss, surplus for muscle gain. BMI indicates your weight category relative to height.

🍽️

Macro Distribution

Our diet plans follow a balanced 30% protein, 40% carbohydrates, and 30% fat split. This distribution supports muscle maintenance, sustained energy, and healthy hormone function across most fitness goals.

💡

Tips for Success

Consistency beats perfection. Track your intake for at least 2 weeks before adjusting. Stay hydrated, prioritize sleep, and remember — these are estimates. Consult a nutritionist for personalized medical advice.
